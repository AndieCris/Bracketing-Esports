# Glossary

## Tournament

A competition that defines the teams, format, schedule, and matches used to determine a champion.

## Team

A tournament entrant with five required starting players, an optional substitute, and a seed used for bracket placement.

## Match

A scheduled contest between two teams. A match is played as a best-of series; its score counts games won, and its winner advances according to the tournament format.

## Series score

The number of games each team wins in a match. A BO1 needs one game win, a BO3 needs two, and a BO5 needs three.

## Bye

An automatic advancement in an elimination bracket when a team has no opponent in that bracket slot. A bye is not a played match and does not count toward match totals.

## Upper bracket and lower bracket

In double elimination, a team enters the lower bracket after its first match loss and is eliminated after its second. The upper-bracket finalist has not yet lost when entering the grand final.

## Grand-final reset

The conditional second grand-final match in double elimination. It is played only when the lower-bracket finalist wins the first grand final.

## Team status

`Registered` means accepted before bracket generation. `Qualified` means still eligible to advance in an elimination format, or still participating in round robin. `Eliminated` means the team has no remaining path to the championship.

## Round-robin tie

Teams tied on match wins are ordered by their head-to-head mini-table, then overall game differential. Any remaining tie is resolved by the organizer.

## Public snapshot

A standalone HTML export of tournament information at the time it was generated. It does not receive later changes from the organizer's browser.
